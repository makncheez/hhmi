For the redesign

Things to look at

  1. Orphan pages. #/janelia/join/opportunities and #/janelia/prototype-guide render, but nothing links to them. They look like leftovers from an earlier navigation.
  2. Two “Other roles” pages. #/careers/other-roles (HHMI) is reachable only from one interest page and just points onward to #/janelia/join/other-roles.
  3. “Explore your path” has no menu home. The careers quiz (6 pages) isn’t under any header item, so no menu item highlights while you’re in it. It’s reached from the HHMI home and Join Janelia.
  4. About HHMI is footer-only. #/about has no header entry and no in-page links pointing to it.
  5. People appear twice. Five people have both a Leadership profile and a Lab page (Spruston, Rubin, Lavis, Saalfeld, Hermundstad). Dudman has only a lab page.
  6. Duplicate URL for one story. The fly connectome story also lives at #/discoveries/fly-connectome, and the Open science page links to that alias.
  7. Label mismatches. #/janelia/about sets the browser tab to “Prototype guide”. Janelia’s Other roles page has the breadcrumb “Students and other roles”.
  8. Thinly linked pages. Data and code, the Dudman lab, and each interest page have only one way in.
Source: HHMI_Janelia_Prototype_9.27.html. Every link the prototype renders was followed in a headless browser. Routes in the code that nothing links to are listed as orphans.
